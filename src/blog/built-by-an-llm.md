This craps table exists because practicing bet strategy shouldn't require a casino, an account, or a wall of ads — just a clean, honest simulation of the real rules: true odds, correct payouts, no house tricks buried in the fine print.

Every part of it — the layout, the styling, the game logic, the odds math — was written entirely by a large language model through nothing more than plain-language conversation, without a developer touching a single line by hand. That includes the [Pass Line and Odds math](/strategy/pass-line-and-odds/), the Place and Buy payout tables, the seven-out resolution logic, and the site you're reading this on right now.

That's the part worth sitting with: a fully playable, rules-accurate casino game, built end-to-end from a back-and-forth chat. It's a small glimpse of where AI-assisted development is headed — not replacing the person with the idea, but shrinking the distance between having one and holding something real.

If you want to see the actual strategy behind the game rather than just play it, start with [bankroll management](/strategy/bankroll-management/) — it's the part most players skip, and the part that decides whether a session is fun or a wipeout.
