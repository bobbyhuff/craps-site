**Quick answer: Not by changing bet sizes or following streaks alone.** A betting system changes **when and how much** you stake. It does not remove the 0 and 00 on an American roulette wheel or improve the payout odds for a standard bet.

## Why the roulette edge persists

American roulette has 38 pockets. Red covers 18. For a $10 even-money bet, expected profit per spin is `(18/38 × $10) − (20/38 × $10) = −$0.5263`. That's a 5.26% average loss per dollar wagered.

No progression makes that equation positive. A system may show many small wins and occasional large losses, or it may create more gradual fluctuations. The underlying expectation remains negative.

## Popular systems compared

| System | Mechanism | Main risk |
|---|---|---|
| Martingale | Double after each loss | Rapid bet escalation; bankroll and table limits |
| Fibonacci | Move through Fibonacci bet sizes after losses | Escalating exposure in long sequences |
| D'Alembert | Increase one unit after a loss, decrease after a win | Bigger stakes during prolonged bad runs |
| Flat betting | Same wager every spin | Still loses the standard house edge over action |
| Streak-following | Bet on recent winning color | Past outcomes don't predict a fair next spin |

## How Martingale can look profitable while losing

Start with a $5 bet on red. After five consecutive losses, you've lost `$5 + $10 + $20 + $40 + $80 = $155`, and the next required bet is **$160**. Winning that next bet earns just **$5 net** for the cycle. A $500 bankroll is exposed to sharp losses for small recurring gains.

## 'But I only need one win'

A win eventually is not guaranteed within your budget or a table limit. American roulette has a 20/38 chance that a red bet loses. Five consecutive losses have probability `(20/38)^5 ≈ 4.04%` for a specified run of five spins. That sounds uncommon until many cycles are played.

## Is European roulette different?

Yes. Its 37 pockets create a standard single-zero house edge of about 2.70%. Playing a better wheel changes the mathematical cost; a betting progression doesn't. Additional rules such as *la partage* can reduce the edge on qualifying even-money bets further.

## FAQ

### Does a strategy help me quit with a profit?

It can affect the distribution of session outcomes, but cannot guarantee profit or remove the house edge.

### Is betting red after five blacks smart?

No better than betting red at another time, assuming independent fair spins.

### Can betting systems change volatility?

Absolutely. Volatility and probability of ruin can change drastically even when the mathematical expectation remains unfavorable.
