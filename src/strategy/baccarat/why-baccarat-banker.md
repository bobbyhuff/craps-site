**Banker is the mathematically strongest of the three common baccarat bets under standard commission rules.** Despite a 5% commission on winning Banker wagers, its house edge is about **1.06%**, compared with about **1.24%** for Player and **14.36%** for an 8:1 Tie bet.

## Banker isn't the person dealing cards

'Banker' and 'Player' label two hands in baccarat. You can bet either side; you do not get to choose when either hand draws. The third-card rules are automatic. Because those rules favor the Banker hand slightly, casinos charge commission on Banker wins to keep the game favorable to the house.

## Standard eight-deck probabilities

| Outcome | Approximate probability |
|---|---:|
| Banker wins | 45.86% |
| Player wins | 44.62% |
| Tie | 9.52% |

For an ordinary Banker or Player wager, a tie is a **push**: you get the wager back. The commission applies only to a winning Banker bet, not to the stake returned on a tie.

## Does a higher win percentage guarantee profit?

No. Even after accounting for ties and Banker commission, the expected return remains slightly negative. Banker has the **lowest average cost**, not a positive expected return under these rules.

## A $100 wager example

A $100 winning Banker wager earns $95 in profit after 5% commission; a losing wager loses $100, and a tied hand returns the original $100. Using approximate standard probabilities, expected profit is about `0.4586 × $95 − 0.4462 × $100`, or approximately **−$1.06** per $100 wagered. Rounded probabilities create a small rounding difference.

## Why not just follow the scoreboard?

A scoreboard records what happened. It doesn't prove that the next hand is 'due' for Player or that a Banker streak will continue. The next hand's probability depends on game rules and shoe composition, not a visual pattern alone.

## When might Player be preferable?

Variants such as no-commission baccarat can change Banker payouts and expected values. Never assume the standard 1.06% figure applies to altered rules. Compare the posted paytable first.

## FAQ

### Is Banker always better than Player?

Under standard baccarat with a 5% commission, it has the lower house edge. Other variants can change this.

### Does Banker win more than half of all hands?

No. Banker wins about 45.86% of all hands, because ties also occur. It wins a slight majority of *non-tied* hands.

### Is Tie worth betting because it pays 8:1?

Its standard house edge is much higher than Banker or Player. The large payout doesn't make it the better mathematical bet.
