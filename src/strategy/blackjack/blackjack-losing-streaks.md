**There is no fixed maximum.** Blackjack can produce long losing streaks, and the length depends on the number of hands played, the rules, player decisions, and whether you count pushes as interruptions.

## Define what you're measuring

A blackjack hand can win, lose, or push. It can also split into multiple decisions. A statement like 'eight straight losses' is ambiguous unless you define whether pushes break the streak and whether split hands count individually. Different rule sets also produce different loss probabilities.

## The probability of a specified losing sequence

For a simple independent Bernoulli model with a fixed per-hand loss probability **p**, the chance of losing **k specific consecutive hands** is `p^k`.

To illustrate only, if **p = 0.48**:

| Consecutive losses | Probability for a specified block |
|---|---:|
| 3 | 11.06% |
| 5 | 2.55% |
| 8 | 0.282% |
| 10 | 0.0649% |

**Important:** Those are illustrative calculations, **not measured blackjack probabilities** for the 0stakes game. Real blackjack hands in a finite shoe are not perfectly independent; doubles, splits, pushes, and strategy change the distribution. The probability of observing at least one streak somewhere in a long session is also **larger** than the probability of a streak in one specified block.

## Why streaks happen even with good strategy

Basic strategy maximizes expected play for the table rules; it cannot control card order. Even strong players experience consecutive losses. A string of losses alone is not evidence the game changed, and a winning streak doesn't show a profitable advantage.

## Should you double after every loss?

A loss doesn't make the next hand 'due' to win. Doubling bets after each loss can cause stakes to rise exponentially, and blackjack doubles and splits can require extra cash. This is a money-management progression, not a method for improving the cards.

## A better way to plan for variance

Choose a fixed session budget and wager amount you can afford to lose. Track total dollars risked, not just how many hands won. ## FAQ

### Is losing five blackjack hands in a row rare?

Not necessarily. The probability depends on the definition of 'loss' and the precise rules; in a long enough sequence, losing streaks are commonplace.

### Are losses independent in blackjack?

Not strictly in a finite shoe without replacement. Shoe composition can matter, but the mere occurrence of a losing streak does not predict the next hand reliably.

### Can perfect basic strategy prevent losing streaks?

No. It helps avoid costly decisions, not ordinary variance.
